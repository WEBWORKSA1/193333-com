The nine-year cycle

Personal Years run in a repeating nine-year cycle: 1 starts something, 2 develops it with others, 3 expresses it, 4 builds the structure, 5 brings change, 6 adds responsibility, 7 reflects, 8 harvests results and 9 completes and releases. Formula: birth month + birth day + the year in question, each reduced, then reduced together.
