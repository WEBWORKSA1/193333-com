How the harmony score works

Numerology traditionally groups the digits into three “families”: 1-5-7 (independent and curious), 2-4-8 (practical and structured) and 3-6-9 (creative and caring). Two numbers from the same family score highest, identical numbers score well but share blind spots, and numbers from different families score as complementary. The score is a conversation starter, not a verdict — real compatibility is built on communication and shared values.
