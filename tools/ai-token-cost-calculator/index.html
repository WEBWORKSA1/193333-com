How to use the estimate

The default prices are placeholders — replace them with the current rates on your provider’s pricing page. Then compare the monthly cost with your revenue per user. A product that costs $0.01 per request and earns $0.002 in ad revenue per request loses money at scale; caching repeated prompts, trimming context and routing simple tasks to smaller models are the usual levers.
