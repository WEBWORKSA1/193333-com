How the calculation works

  1. Reduce the month: December (12) becomes 1 + 2 = 3.
  2. Reduce the day: 25 becomes 2 + 5 = 7. Days 11, 22 and 29 (2 + 9 = 11) keep their master value.
  3. Reduce the year: 1990 becomes 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19, then 1 + 9 = 10, then 1.
  4. Add the three: 3 + 7 + 1 = 11. Because 11 is a master number, it stays 11.
