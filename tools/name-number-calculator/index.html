What each number means

  • Expression (Destiny) — all letters. Your natural talents and the way you tend to work.
  • Soul Urge (Heart’s Desire) — vowels only. What motivates you privately.
  • Personality — consonants only. The first impression you give others.

Each letter maps to a digit: A, J and S are 1; B, K and T are 2; and so on through I and R at 9. The totals are reduced to a single digit, except for master numbers 11, 22 and 33.
