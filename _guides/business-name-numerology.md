## How to calculate a business name number

Using the Pythagorean chart, convert each letter to a digit (A, J, S = 1; B, K, T = 2; C, L, U = 3; D, M, V = 4; E, N, W = 5; F, O, X = 6; G, P, Y = 7; H, Q, Z = 8; I, R = 9), add them, and reduce the total to a single digit, keeping 11, 22 and 33. Use the name customers see and say, usually without legal suffixes. Our [business name calculator](/tools/business-name-calculator/) does this instantly and shows the working.

## What the numbers are associated with

- **1** — founders, first movers, bold brands
- **2** — partnerships, agencies, service
- **3** — media, creative and marketing businesses
- **4** — finance, construction, logistics: reliability
- **5** — travel, sales, e-commerce: speed and change
- **6** — health, home, hospitality: care
- **7** — research, technology, analytics: expertise
- **8** — finance, real estate, scale: authority
- **9** — non-profits, education, global brands: purpose
- **11, 22, 33** — visionary platforms, large builds, mission-driven organisations

## The checks that matter more

1. **Trademark clearance** in your class and markets.
2. **Domain availability** — ideally the exact-match .com.
3. **Radio test** — can someone spell it after hearing it once?
4. **Cultural check** — meanings and sounds in your target languages.
5. **Social handles** — consistent across platforms.

Only then compare name numbers between the finalists. If two names pass every practical check, letting numerology break the tie costs nothing — and gives the brand a story.
