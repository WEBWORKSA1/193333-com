October 2026: The 193333 Challenge. Entries close at 23:59 UTC on the last day of the month. Winners are announced on this page within 7 days.

This month’s puzzles

  1. The three primes. 193333 is the product of three primes. What is the sum of those three primes?
  2. Master sum. How many six-digit numbers made only of the digits 1, 9 and 3 — using each of them at least once — have a digit sum of exactly 22? (193333 is one of them.)
  3. Base jump. Write 3333 in hexadecimal.
  4. Tiebreaker. In 50 words or fewer: what would you build on a website called 193333?
