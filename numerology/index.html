How numerology reduction works

Numerology reduces numbers by adding their digits until a single digit remains — except for the master numbers 11, 22 and 33, which are kept. Take the domain number itself: 1 + 9 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 22, a master number, so 193333 is read as a "Master Builder" number. The same digit-sum rule is a genuine mathematical property: the digital root of any whole number equals its remainder when divided by 9 (with 9 in place of 0), which is why casting out nines has been used to check arithmetic for centuries.
