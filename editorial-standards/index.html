--- title: "Editorial Standards and Methodology" short: Editorial standards crumb: Editorial standards eyebrow: How we work heading: "Editorial standards & methodology" lead: "How we research, calculate, review and correct everything we publish." description: "193333 editorial standards: sourcing, calculation methods, numerology conventions, review process, corrections policy and advertising independence." --- ## Sourcing Factual claims about mathematics, history, economics and technology are checked against primary or authoritative sources — government publications, academic references, official documentation. Where we summarise others’ work, we paraphrase and link. ## Calculation methods - **Numerology:** Pythagorean letter values; month, day and year reduced separately for Life Path; master numbers 11, 22 and 33 preserved. - **Number facts:** computed exactly in your browser for whole numbers up to 253 − 1. - **Financial calculators:** standard published formulas, shown on each page. Results are educational estimates. ## Tone We present numerology and angel numbers respectfully as reflective traditions, never as predictions or guarantees, and we always show the real mathematics alongside the symbolism. ## Review and updates Pages are reviewed by the 193333 Editorial Team before publication and re-checked when formulas, facts or conventions change. Updated dates are shown on each page. ## Corrections Found an error? Tell us via the [contact page](/contact/) (topic: “Correction or feedback”). We fix confirmed errors promptly and note significant corrections on the page. ## Independence Advertising and sponsorship never influence calculator results or readings. Sponsored placements are always labelled.
